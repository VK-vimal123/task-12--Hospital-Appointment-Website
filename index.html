About MediCare Plus

Designed Around Calm, Clarity, and Clinical Precision

Founded with a vision to bridge world-class medical technology with genuine human warmth, MediCare Plus serves families with integrated outpatient clinics, advanced diagnostic laboratories, and specialized surgical care under one roof.
